# How I helped expand one of Oregon States largest Computer Science clubs from 5 to 12 Officers

ACM (Associationof Computer Machinery) is one of Oregon State's largest Computer Science clubs, we meet Thursdays from 6-7pm (feel free to stop by for free pizza, link on the homepage 😁). During the 2023-24 school year ACM was going through a period of change. An almost entirely new officer team was a great indicator of strong enthusiasm. Our ambitions for the year were quickly brought to a head with simply the lack of time. Our goals were ambitious, host 9 general meetings a term and 9 competitive programming meeting a week. Within the first 2 weeks of term we hit a roadblock. Our primary officer for competitive programming couldn't continue hosting 2 events a week.

It's around this point when I took our president aside and discussed the idea of expanding the offer board. At the time we only had 5 officers. A small team kept the meetings intimate and it was fun, but we couldn't do any of the ambitious events we had planned. Over the span of a month in the cold of November, me and our president discussed ideas on how many new people we should bring onboard, what their roles should be, what the roles of our current officerboard should shift to if anything different. Lots of revisions came and went, but we settled on a total of 12 postions.

Our new roles went as follows

- President
- Vice President
- Treasurer
- Career Development Officer
- 2 community outreach officers
- 2 competitive programming officers
- tech lab officer
- web master
- online outreach officer
- graphics design officer

As the 2024-25 year has begun, we've found a revived interest in the club from our members and officers. Knowing that I had such a large impact on the club has been a major part of my happiness this year.
