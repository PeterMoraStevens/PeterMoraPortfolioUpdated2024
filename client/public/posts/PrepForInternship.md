# I start my internship @ Netflix in 3 days; here's what I've done to prepare

## Outline

* People I've talked with
* Skills I've been practicing
* The most important step of all

### The People I've Talked With

Learning from those who have been where you are is always a good idea. When I think of who I should meet, I thought immediately of previous interns. I met with a previous Netflix intern who went fulltime from my sister team. We had some great discussions on how to succeed at Netflix. More importantly though we discussed how to thrive in life around the Bay and especially in Big Tech. Some key take aways from that discussion I think were

1. If you are going to prepare for anything, look at the language you'll use and get familiar with the syntax, though that is easy enough on the job
2. Practicing some system design wouldn't hurt, often companies at the scale of Netflix will have you encountering these types of problems and it could be helpful to have read up on them beforehand

I think across the past month or so I've met with my Manager 3x times. This amongst my other meetings has been the most calming of any other. Getting to know the person I would be working with has been really enjoyable.

### The Skills I've been Practicing

Since my team is responsible for Android Playback I've been working mostly on learning the Kotlin Syntax. I specifically worked on basic class logic, loops, and multithreading. Depending on the company, and in my case at Netflix, your manager might share some ideas of what to prepare before you start.

### The most important step of all

Meet the other interns! Get excited to work, hangout, and have fun the entire summer! I've been lucky enough to have multiple internship experiences in the Bay Area. I found the previous summer 2024 to be difficult due to my office having very few interns. When you have the ability to hangout with interns and New Grads, take every opportunity you can.
