### The People I've Talked With

Learning from those who have been where you are is always a good idea. When I think of who I should meet, I thoughtimmediately of previous interns. I met with a previous Netflix intern who went fulltime from my sister team. We had some great discussions on how to succeed at Netflix. More importantly though we discussed how to thrive in life around the Bay and especially in Big Tech. Some key take aways from that discussion I think were

1. If you are going to prepare for anything, look at the language you'll use and get familiar with the syntax, though that is easy enough on the job
3. Practicing some system design wouldn't hurt, often companies at the scale of Netflix will have you encountering these types of problems and it could be helpful to have read up on them beforehand

I think across the past month or so I've met with my Manager 3x times. This amongst my other meetings has been the most calming of any other. Getting to know the person I would be working with has been really enjoyable.
